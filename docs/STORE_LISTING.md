## Short description (80 chars max, Play)

```
Shrink PDFs to an exact size, ready for job, visa and gov uploads. Offline.
```

## Full description (Play, 4000 chars max)

```
PDF Fit does one job properly: it makes a PDF small enough — and correct enough — to actually be accepted by the portal you are uploading to.

Set the target size you need (100 KB, 200 KB, 500 KB, 1 MB, 2 MB, or any custom size) and PDF Fit measures the real output until it meets it. You see the true before-and-after size, the resolution used, and every attempt that was tried. No fake "done!" message.

WHY PDF FIT IS DIFFERENT

• Real target sizes, verified. The app rebuilds the PDF and measures the file on disk. If a target cannot be reached, it says so instead of pretending.
• Upload readiness checker. Pick the portal type — job application, government form, visa, scholarship, email — and PDF Fit opens your file and checks the actual requirements: file size, page count, A4 page size, portrait orientation, uniform page sizes, presence of a text layer (scanned vs text), annotations and password protection.
• Everything stays on your phone. Your documents are never uploaded. No account, no sign-in, no cloud.

DOCUMENT TOOLS

• Compress to a target size (JPEG quality and DPI chosen automatically, with a full audit trail of attempts)
• Merge several PDFs into one, in any order
• Split: every N pages, one file per page, odd/even pages, or exact page ranges
• Rotate pages 90°, 180° or 270° — all pages or just the ones that are sideways
• Images to PDF: photos and scans from your gallery, with A4/Letter/A5 or fit-to-image page sizes

BUILT FOR REAL UPLOADS

Job portals, government forms, university admissions, visa applications and email attachments all have size and format limits. PDF Fit tells you exactly which requirement fails and offers the tool that fixes it.

PRIVACY FIRST

• Documents are processed on-device with an embedded PDF engine
• No document upload, no analytics, no account
• Your original files are never modified — results are always new copies
• Clear your history in one tap

FREE AND PREMIUM

The free (Basic) plan includes compression with common target sizes, merging two documents, splitting by page count, rotating, and up to five images per PDF.

Premium unlocks custom target sizes, unlimited compression, unlimited merges, exact page ranges, unlimited images, unlimited presets, full history and removes ads. One purchase — not a subscription.

Note: Premium is a one-time in-app purchase handled by the store. Prices are shown by the store before you pay.

QUESTIONS

PDF Fit processes everything locally, so it works without an internet connection (except for ads in the free version and the purchase flow).
```
